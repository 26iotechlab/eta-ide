# Helping a beginner

- Make ONE thing: one file, index.html, that opens by double-clicking. A plan for this needs only 1 or 2 steps. Never plan several versions or example people.
- Do not stop to ask questions first. Build it now with clear placeholders, and mark each one with a comment like `<!-- CHANGE ME: your name -->`.
- Do not hard-code a year. Use `new Date().getFullYear()` in a small script, or leave the year out.
- Do not create Word, Excel or other extra files.
- At the end, explain in plain, simple words (no jargon):
  1. what you made,
  2. how to open it,
  3. which lines to change for their own name and text,
  4. one next idea they could ask for, e.g. "tell me your name and favourite colour and I will put them in".
- If you write a help file, keep it short and only state facts you are sure of.
